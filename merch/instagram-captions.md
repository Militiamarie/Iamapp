# Instagram Captions — Last Stand Drop

## Launch Post (whole drop)

The Last Stand drop is live. 🖤

This isn't a fashion line. It's a memorial, a warning, and a promise.

The Origin Piece — a Native woman's face in graffiti linework, red paint dripping like tears, one eye staring out. Drawn from my own tribe's visual language, by my own hand. She's the center of everything.

The Lion — red tears, "The Blood of the People" across the back. The rage that answers the grief.

The Trail of Tears Jacket — the full map of the forced removal routes on the back, the Oregon route to Grand Ronde on the sleeve. "They walked. We remember."

The MMIW Tank — red handprint over the heart, red line down the side seam. A portion of proceeds goes to Ampkwa Advocacy, a Grand Ronde-based nonprofit doing the ground work in our own community.

The LOVE Piece — pink dripping graffiti, the same hand as the red eye. The love that survives everything.

Five pieces. One story. Our ancestors walked through hell and we're still here — still creating, still remembering, still standing.

## Heritage Line

The heritage line is coming. 🖤

I Am isn't just tactical streetwear — it's culture you can wear. Moccasins, beaded headbands, armbands, and obsidian arrowhead earrings, made by Grand Ronde hands.

The moccasins carry patterns passed down through generations. The turquoise headbands and armbands are everyday pieces that keep the connection alive. The obsidian arrowheads are carved from volcanic glass — the same material our ancestors used for tools and ceremony thousands of years ago.

Every maker will be credited by name. This isn't borrowed imagery — it's inherited.
