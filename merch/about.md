# About — I Am / Mad Souls Family

I Am is a Native streetwear line built by Melitia Marie, a member of the Confederated Tribes of Grand Ronde. The name says it all — this is who we are, not who we're trying to be.

The line moves between two worlds: tactical streetwear built for the end of the world, and heritage pieces rooted in the culture that survived it. The Trail of Tears Jacket carries the map of the forced removal routes. The MMIW tank honors the women who were taken. The moccasins, headbands, and obsidian earrings carry the traditions that kept us alive.

A portion of proceeds from the MMIW piece supports Ampkwa Advocacy, a Grand Ronde-based nonprofit working to bring our missing home.

We are still here. We are still creating. We are still standing.
