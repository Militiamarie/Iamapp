# Pep Boys Lien Warning — Act Now

**Vehicle:** Work vehicle at Pep Boys
**Days in storage:** 14 (two weeks)
**Amount:** $1,700.00

## The risk

After roughly 30 days in storage, a shop can file a **mechanic's lien** against the vehicle. Once a lien is filed, the title is clouded — you cannot sell, transfer, or register the car cleanly, and the lien follows the vehicle. Getting it released after that is a legal fight, not a phone call.

Two weeks in means you have roughly two weeks left before that window closes. This is not a someday bill. This is a this-week bill.

## What to do today

1. Call Pep Boys and ask for the itemized balance in writing — every charge, every day of storage.
2. Ask if they will accept a partial payment or payment arrangement to stop storage fees from accruing.
3. Pay what you can today. Even $850 stops the clock on part of it.
4. Get a written release commitment before you hand over the final payment.
5. Verify the vehicle is drivable before you leave the lot.

## Why this matters for Mad Souls Family

The work vehicle is the mobility of the operation — studio runs, equipment, deliveries, in-person meetings with artisans and partners. A lien on the title would cripple the brand's ability to move.
